## Homework 4 --- Risk Management and Communication Planning

**Prompt used:**

> Can you explain the difference between technical, schedule, financial, and people risks using examples from our Smart Parking Platform?

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT explained the four required risk categories in the context of the existing project. Technical risks were connected to APIs, real-time availability, security, and system integration; schedule risks to dependencies, testing, and scope changes; financial risks to service costs and rework; and people risks to availability, skill gaps, communication, and workload.

**What was retained or changed:**  
The explanation was used to organize the Homework 4 risk identification section. At least four project-specific risks were retained for each required category.

**Prompt used:**

> How do probability and impact work in a risk register, and how should I decide whether a risk is low, medium, high, or extreme?

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT explained probability as the likelihood that a risk will occur and impact as the effect on project objectives if it occurs. The explanation followed the course 1-5 probability and impact scales and the risk-matrix concept.

**What was retained or changed:**  
A 1-5 probability and impact approach was retained for the project Risk Register. Priority labels were assigned using the course risk-matrix framework and reviewed for consistency with each risk description.

**Prompt used:**

> Explain the difference between avoid, mitigate, transfer, and accept risk responses, and give me Smart Parking Platform examples.

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT explained the four response strategies and connected them to the project: avoiding unnecessary scope changes, mitigating integration/security/schedule risks, transferring appropriate external responsibilities to service providers when applicable, and accepting low-priority risks when additional action would not be justified.

**What was retained or changed:**  
Avoid and Mitigate were used for the risks in the submitted Risk Register because they best matched the selected project risks. Transfer and Accept remain valid response options but were not forced into the register where they did not fit.

**Prompt used:**

> How should a communication plan differ for developers, project managers, executives, and other stakeholders?

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT explained that communication should be tailored to the audience. Development teams need frequent task-level and technical information, project management needs schedule/risk/decision visibility, and stakeholders need concise milestone, metric, risk, and decision reporting.

**What was retained or changed:**  
The Communication Plan was organized by audience/team, cadence, participants, method, primary content, and owner. Daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, and milestone-based communication were assigned based on the needs of each group.

**Prompt used:**

> Using my existing Smart Parking Platform project, help me review whether my risks, risk register, and communication plan are consistent with the scope and schedule I already created.

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT reviewed the new Homework 4 material against the existing project scope, requirements, backlog, Sprint 1, semester schedule, third-party dependencies, security/privacy constraints, and final QA/release activities.

**What was retained or changed:**  
Risks and communication activities were aligned with existing project elements rather than introducing unrelated features. Examples include third-party API dependencies, real-time availability, authentication/security, payment integration, operator/garage management, QA/security testing, and the semester release timeline.

**Prompt used:**

> Using my completed Homework 4 content, polish the structure, vocabulary, tables, headings, and formatting so it matches the professional style of my existing project document.

**How AI was used:**  
ChatGPT assisted with final document editing and presentation, including section numbering, wording, table structure, headings, page layout, headers/footers, and consistency with the earlier project-management-plan format.

**What was retained or changed:**  
The Homework 4 content was integrated as Sections 8-10 of Version 0.4. The existing project content and decisions were preserved while the new Risk Management, Risk Register, and Communication Plan sections were formatted as part of the same professional project document.
